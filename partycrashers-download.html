Why Saturn Players Remember It

  • CD audio music: the soundtrack is honestly one of the best parts.
  • Big chunky Saturn look: lots of dark overlays, bright UI, and stage art that looks great on a blurry TV.
  • Fast rounds: easy to restart, easy to get mad, easy to play "one more" for way too long.
  • Different revisions: some people still swear Rev A feels different. maybe they're right.
